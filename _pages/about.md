I am a first-year PhD student in the Department of Statistical Science at University College London (UCL), funded through the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) in [Collaborative Computational Modelling at the Interface](https://ccmi-cdt.org/). I am a member of the [Fundamentals of Statistical Machine Learning](https://fsml-ucl.github.io/) group. I am supervised by [François-Xavier Briol](https://fxbriol.github.io/), [Matt Graham](https://matt-graham.github.io), [Jeremias Knoblauch](https://jeremiasknoblauch.github.io) and [Louis Sharrock](https://louissharrock.github.io). My current research focuses on simulation-based inference and predictively-oriented inference.


# Background

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/) and a Master's degree in Mathematics from the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.cit.tum.de/en/cit/home/). My Master's thesis, supervised by Prof. [Andreas S. Schulz](https://www.ot.mgt.tum.de/en/or/group/andreas-s-schulz/), focused on stochastic online scheduling on parallel machines.

Prior to starting my PhD, I worked for two years as a Mathematical Consultant at the [Smith Institute](https://www.smithinst.co.uk) in Oxford, where I applied mathematical modelling and machine learning to real-world problems for industry and government clients. During my studies, I have also gained experience as a Data Analytics intern at [Munich Re](https://www.munichre.com/en.html) and as a research assistant at the [Fraunhofer Institute for Cognitive Systems (IKS)](https://www.iks.fraunhofer.de/en.html) in the Reasoned AI for Healthcare group.
